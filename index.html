A LITTLE ABOUT US

From Russia, with
student solidarity.

MedRaahi began with a simple thought: studying medicine abroad can feel overwhelming, and finding well-organised learning resources shouldn't make it harder.

Built by an Indian medical student living in Russia, this project aims to bring useful notes, legally shareable PDFs and question-paper resources together in one friendly place.
